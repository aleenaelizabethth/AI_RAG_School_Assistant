EXCELLENT PUBLIC SCHOOL

School Policies, Facilities and Student Life

Fictional portfolio demonstration document — not official school regulations.

1. School Timings

The regular school day runs from 8:30 AM to 12:45 PM, Monday through Friday. Students are expected to arrive before classes begin and follow the timetable provided by the school.

2. Attendance

Regular attendance and punctuality are expected of every student. Parents or guardians should inform the school office when a student is absent. Attendance records and individual attendance queries are handled through the appropriate school staff.

3. Leave Policy

Parents or guardians should notify the school office about planned or unexpected absences. Leave requests are subject to the school's applicable procedures and approval requirements.

4. Academic Programme and Examinations

Students participate in the academic programme according to their grade and timetable. Examination schedules, assessment procedures, individual results and academic support are managed by the relevant teachers and academic department.

5. Sports and Physical Education

Excellent Public School provides a broad range of sporting and physical education opportunities. Facilities and activities may include:

- Swimming pool and swimming training
- Badminton courts and coaching
- Cricket grounds, practice facilities and equipment
- Football grounds and team training
- Tennis courts and coaching
- General physical education, fitness activities and other sports programmes

Students are expected to participate in at least one organised sports or physical activity, subject to applicable medical, safety and accessibility considerations. Students should follow the instructions of sports instructors and use facilities under appropriate supervision.

Training schedules, team selection, facility access, equipment requirements, swimming arrangements, eligibility and programme availability should be confirmed with the Sports Department.

6. Extracurricular Activities and Performing Arts

The school encourages students to discover their interests, develop confidence and build creative skills through extracurricular activities, including:

- Dance and choreography
- Singing, choir and vocal music
- Guitar and other musical instruments
- Drama, theatre and stage performance
- Skits, mime and storytelling
- Public speaking and cultural programmes
- Art, creative activities and other student clubs

Every student is expected to participate in at least one extracurricular activity in addition to sports, subject to individual needs and applicable school arrangements. Students may choose additional activities based on their interests and availability.

Activity schedules, auditions, rehearsals, instructors, equipment, participation requirements and event arrangements are managed by the relevant activity coordinators. Please contact the school office for information about the appropriate coordinator.

7. School Transport and Bus Facilities

School bus facilities are available as part of the school's transport arrangements. Routes, pickup and drop-off points, timings, seat availability, transport fees, safety procedures and registration requirements depend on the applicable arrangements.

For route details, availability, registration, transport charges or changes to an existing arrangement, contact the Transportation Department through the school office. Do not assume a route or seat is available until it has been confirmed by the department.

8. Student Conduct and Responsibilities

Students are expected to:

- Treat classmates, teachers, coaches and other staff respectfully.
- Take care of classrooms, sports facilities, musical instruments and school property.
- Follow instructions and safety procedures during classes, sports training and extracurricular activities.
- Use sports and activity equipment responsibly.
- Participate constructively in academic, sporting and cultural activities.

9. Sports and Activity Safety

Students must follow the safety instructions provided by qualified staff. Swimming, sports training and the use of specialist facilities require compliance with the relevant supervision and safety arrangements. Any medical, accessibility or other participation concern should be discussed with the school office and the appropriate department.

10. School Fees and Accounts

For tuition fees, transport charges, activity fees, payment deadlines, outstanding balances, refunds or receipts, contact the Accounts Department through the school office. Specific amounts and deadlines must be confirmed using authorised school information.

11. School Facilities and General Enquiries

The school offers academic, sporting, cultural and student-development facilities. Access, schedules, availability, eligibility and applicable charges may vary by programme and grade.

For detailed information about facilities, admissions, departments, events, programmes or services, contact the school office directly by telephone or email.

12. Contact and Departmental Assistance

The school office is the main point of contact for directing enquiries to the appropriate department.

- Academic enquiries: Academic Department or relevant teacher
- Sports and coaching: Sports Department
- Dance, music, drama and other activities: Relevant activity coordinator
- Bus routes and transport arrangements: Transportation Department
- Fees and payments: Accounts Department
- Attendance and student administration: School office or authorised administrative staff
